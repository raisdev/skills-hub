Installing a plugin

  1. Open Claude Desktop, then Settings, Plugins, Organization.
  2. Choose the RAIS Skills Hub tab.
  3. Click Install on the ones you want. Their skills work in Cowork and Chat right away.

Nothing installs on its own. Updates to a plugin you installed arrive automatically. If the tab is missing, your PC has not had the marketplace update yet; ask RAIS.
