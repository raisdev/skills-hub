## 3. Merchandising

- **GOV-20 [Official]** Buy Cornell-branded items (apparel, gifts, sporting goods) from the Cornell Store or a **licensed manufacturer**. Licensing is administered by Exemplar Associates and includes a code of conduct against sweatshop conditions. Only officially licensed vendors may produce items with Cornell marks, and all designs are reviewed before production. (https://brand.cornell.edu/merchandising; licensee guide)
- **GOV-21 [Official]** On merchandise, the logo must be rendered in **Cornell red (PMS 187), white or black**. It may be etched on metal or glass or stamped on metal or leather.
- **GOV-22 [Official]** For embroidery, the seal should be at least **2-5/16" in diameter**.
- **GOV-23 [Official]** Do not redraw, reconstruct or modify the logo in any way.
- **GOV-24 [Official]** The licensee guide requires approval, by the same process as logos, for the words "Cornell University", "Cornell Big Red" and the college, school and program names listed in it. It states Location: Ithaca, NY; Established: 1865. It gives Cornell Red as Pantone 187c, RGB 170/20/45 (see SRC-03) and Black as CMYK 0/0/0/100.
- **GOV-25 [Official]** The Block C is for athletic branded products only and is not combined with "Cornell University", "Cornell Alumni" or "Established 1865". The C Bear works as a spirit mark and can be combined with "Cornell University" and "Cornell Alumni". Retro Bear marks are "very limited". (Licensee guide, August 2024.)
- **GOV-26 [Official]** Trademark symbols: see `logos.md`, LOG-70 to LOG-72.

## 4. FAQ rules

Source: https://brand.cornell.edu/resources/faqs.

- **GOV-30 [Official]** All colleges, departments and units must follow the brand guidelines, for consistency and clarity.
- **GOV-31 [Official]** Altering or editing Cornell's marks is prohibited (Policy 4.10).
- **GOV-32 [Official]** Departments, programs, centers and institutes "may not have logos" (see SRC-05 for the conflict with the brand architecture page).
- **GOV-33 [Official]** "Do I have to use red? Yes." First-and-only rule (COL-01).
- **GOV-34 [Official]** All websites on the cornell.edu domain must follow the brand guidelines and Cornell's web standards. IT provides free WordPress brand-compliant templates (https://it.cornell.edu/blogs). Sites hosted outside cornell.edu should not display a Cornell logo or seal (LOG-03).
- **GOV-35 [Official]** Logo or seal in an email signature: unnecessary (LOG-05).
- **GOV-36 [Official]** Vendor use of the logo on its website: submit the Use of Name or Logo form; Brand Communications approves.
- **GOV-37 [Official]** Each college or school has a communications team with the assets. Directory: https://universityrelations.cornell.edu/resources/comms-leads/.

## 5. Terminology

Source: https://brand.cornell.edu/resources/terminology. Definitions worth knowing when reading brand guidance: *branded house* (Cornell's model) vs *house of brands* (prohibited); *lockup logo* (logo combined with a department name in set arrangements); *responsive logo* (renders well across device sizes); *supergraphic* (a single graphic or pattern for impact that "must be used carefully to avoid undermining existing logos"); *workmark* (the Cornell name in a distinctive typographic style, type only); *endorsement*; *co-branding*; *design system* ("a collection of unique, reusable components, guided by clear standards") . Email brand@cornell.edu with questions.

- **GOV-40 [Official]** The site uses "design system" only as a definition. It does not publish a Cornell design system or component library. (Unspecified beyond that.)

## 6. When to hand off to a person

Stop and send the user to Brand Communications (consultation form or brand@cornell.edu) instead of deciding, when the request involves:

- creating, altering, recoloring or recreating any Cornell logo, seal, lockup or wordmark (GOV-31, LOG-01)
- a new logo or distinct identity for a department, program, center, institute, lab, initiative, event or group (LOG-52 to LOG-56)
- Cornell logo on a non-cornell.edu site or a vendor's site (LOG-03, GOV-36)
- merchandise, or production of a branded physical item (GOV-11, GOV-20)
- affiliates, co-branding or endorsement (LOG-41)
- student-organization, alumni-club or other names under Policy 4.10 (NOM-60, NOM-65)
- any name not covered by the nomenclature table (NOM-50)
- publishing a photo or video of a person without a release, or any image of a minor (MED-51)
- accessibility exceptions or documented "undue burden" (GOV-07)
- questions that need the full text of Policy 4.10 or 5.12 (not in this KB)
